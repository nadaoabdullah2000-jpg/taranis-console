1. Summary

Executive summary

  • TMS shows the least on its product page. Behind its professional-investor gate there is no performance, ISIN, fees, AUM, documents or team, and the two sleeves (systematic sentiment + discretionary AI-disruption) are not stated. Schroders/Contour and UBP/Shannon River show full performance behind the same kind of Swiss gate; AtonRâ, BIT and DECALIA's factsheets show it openly.
  • Taranis posts least among active peers. The company page posted 2 times in 13 weeks; DECALIA, the Swiss boutique bar, posted 30. Contour, Jericho and Shannon River post nothing (confirmed by live search), so the international TMT long/short channel is empty.
  • People carry reach, not pages. Symbit's page got 0 reactions on news its co-founder's reshare turned into 108. DECALIA's page best is 63; its CIO's own post reached 153. Julien's own posts get 48–64; the Taranis page's reshares of them get 14.
  • Monchau is a curation engine. About 81 posts a week, 86% crediting a "Source". His own-view and Syz-branded posts clear 54 reactions 5% of the time; curated ones 59%. Copy his chart-first format, not his volume.
  • The Swiss tech-AI thesis lane is open. AtonRâ writes the best original tech theses but gets 0–10 reactions; Pictet's tech PMs post nothing; DECALIA's feed is 13% tech. A named founder with TV airtime can own it.

Caveats

  • The Crustdata index ends around 17 Sep 2026. LinkedIn's login wall blocked a live refresh with TinyFish, so posts after mid-September are not counted.
  • AtonRâ reads 0 posts in the window, but a live company post dated 30 Sep 2026 and nine research notes from July to September exist; the index misses it. Its 2025 same-window rate (15 posts) is shown as its estimated cadence.
  • Contour, Jericho and Shannon River read 0 and live web search found no posts of their own: they are genuinely quiet, not under-indexed.
  • Pictet and DECALIA counts include corporate content (results, hires, interns, events). Topic shares are keyword matches and overlap.
  • Index dates can drift by up to four weeks; true dates are decoded from LinkedIn post IDs. The index's "repost" label is not a curation signal; "Source" credits are used instead.
  • Post images are linked, not embedded. Some figures in the brief could not be reproduced and are labelled where shown (Monchau's Sep topic counts and format medians; the "Anthropic conspiracy theories" post was not found in the index).

7. SWOT and operating plan

Strengths

  • The only tech long/short with a behavioural-sentiment quant overlay in the set
  • Founder-CEO with BFM Business airtime; his own posts beat the page
  • Award history; clear AI-disruption thesis

Weaknesses

  • Lowest cadence among active peers; page mirrors TV clips
  • Recent posts French-only; ~2,000 followers
  • Product page shows nothing; award label says "Managed Futures"

Opportunities

  • Close competitors are silent on LinkedIn
  • No Swiss tech-AI personal voice with reach
  • A proprietary sentiment read no peer can copy

Threats

  • BIT and AtonRâ already tell the human + AI story with numbers
  • Symbit occupies "Swiss + AI + long/short"
  • FinSA limits public performance talk
