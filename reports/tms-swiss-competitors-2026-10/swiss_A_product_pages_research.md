## (ii) Positioning summaries

**AtonRâ Partners – AI & Robotics AMC** (https://www.atonra.ch/investment-funds/ai-robotics)
AtonRâ sells thematic tech as a range of 12+ single-theme AMCs: AI & Robotics, Security & Space, China Tech, Cybersecurity, Fintech and others, plus a UCITS umbrella fund and a CIO "Top Selection" certificate. The pitch rests on research heritage (equity research since 2004, thematic since 2014). It also leans on a proprietary 1,300-category taxonomy and AI/quant tooling (the Fundy platform). Each product page is open with no gate and shows the price, YTD and ISIN. The linked factsheet gives monthly net returns and fees (1.30% + 0.20% + 15% performance fee). The tone is marketing-heavy and pitched broadly ("seasoned investors to newcomers"). Its most recent push is Fundy-built systematic AMCs, e.g. World Multi-Factor with ZKB, launched 29 Sep 2026.

**DECALIA – Future SOCIETY** (https://www.decalia.com/en-ch/asset-management/strategies/ ; factsheet https://funds-static.pictet.com/funds/decalia/factsheet/MR_ES_en_LU2407358063_YES_2026-05-31.pdf)
Future SOCIETY is a long-only, multi-thematic global growth UCITS fund that leans into tech (about 52% IT, about 35% AI infrastructure). It has a deliberate mid-cap and healthcare barbell. Since inception (Nov 2021) it is EUR 139m and still trails the MSCI World. In 2026 it is a top-decile performer, and DECALIA is promoting it hard (finews interview republished 20 Oct 2026). Corporate pages are multilingual and narrative. Fund data sits behind an investor-category and country gate on a separate SICAV site, but full-performance factsheets leak publicly via Pictet's fund-static CDN. Lead manager Quirien Lemey is the face of the fund. Co-lead Alexander Roose left in April 2026 and Iana Perova was promoted to co-PM.

**Pictet AM – Robotics / Digital / TR-Atlas Titan** (https://am.pictet.com/ch/en/intermediaries/funds/pictet-robotics/LU1279334210)
Pictet is the scale benchmark: Robotics alone is EUR 13bn, and the firm runs 30 years of thematic franchise across CHF 285bn of AUM. Every share class has a standardised, data-dense page with live NAV, fund size, SRI and benchmark-relative performance. A full regulatory document library (KID, prospectus, reports, SFDR) is one click away. Tone is regulatory and technical. Differentiation is high-conviction stock picking within a defined universe, plus ESG (Art. 8). The closest Taranis analogue is TR-Atlas Titan, a discretionary global L/S with a top-down overlay (EUR 688m). It is not tech-specific and is marketed with awards and a stated return/volatility target.

**Taranis – Market Sentiment (TMS)** (https://taranis.net/investment/tms)
TMS is the only tech-focused long/short product in this set. It is also the only one that claims a behavioural/sentiment-driven quant overlay, which is a genuine positioning gap against the long-only thematic peers. But the page gives no ISIN, vehicle, fees, AUM, performance, documents or team, and the only CTA is contact. Its information density is far below even AtonRâ's open pages. The page is gated to professionals in CH/UK. The sole proof point is a 2023 HFM "Managed Futures Newcomer" award, which also sits awkwardly with the L/S-equity tech label. Note: the plain-fetch /investment intro now says TMS "uses alternative data and a systematic overlay" (fetched 27 Oct). That differs slightly from the gated page wording, which says fundamental stock picking plus a quant overlay.
