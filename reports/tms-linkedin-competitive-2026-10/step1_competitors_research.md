# Step 1: Closest competitors to Taranis Market Sentiment (TMS)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Tools used: WebSearch, TinyFish search/fetch, and 3 TinyFish browser-automation runs to get past investor gates. No Crustdata.

**What TMS is, for comparison.** TMS is Taranis SA's long/short equity strategy in technology: "semiconductors, AI infrastructure, space, defense tech and selected hardware". It "combines fundamental stock picking with a quantitative overlay on risk appetite and positioning". The fund page is only for "qualified and professional investors who are resident in Switzerland and United Kingdom". Sources: https://taranis.net/investment, https://taranis.net/investment/tms (behind the gate).

## Why the ranking falls this way
- **#1 BIT**: It is the only competitor that publicly markets the same mix as TMS, a systematic or alternative-data layer plus discretionary tech-sector investing, in a vehicle for professional investors that can short. Its sector focus also matches TMS (semis, AI infrastructure).
- **#2 Contour**: It matches TMS best on product type (tech long/short UCITS for European and Swiss professional investors) and is the most visible rival in UCITS long/short tech. It is purely discretionary.
- **#3 Symbit**: It matches on Swiss domicile, hybrid AI and discretionary process, long/short structure and professional-investor focus. It is generalist and too new to have a track record.
- **#4 Shannon River UCITS**: A tech long/short UCITS on a Swiss bank platform, similar in size to TMS. Fundamental only.
- **#5 Jericho**: The best-known TMT long/short name and a benchmark, but a different scale and market.

## What I could not verify
- **BIT Global Technology Opportunities fund size**: the ~EUR 685m figure comes from aggregators that show no as-of date. BIT's own page loads the number by script and I could not read it.
- **Shannon River UCITS AUM**: UBP shows USD 21.37m but does not say whether that is the sub-fund or the share class. It matches the I-class volume on finanzen.ch, so it may be share-class level.
- **Symbit**: no AUM is disclosed. Also, the LEI record describes the fund as an Irish ICAV, while the press calls it an AIF; that may not be a real conflict.
- **Contour LinkedIn company page**: not found. LinkedIn blocks automated fetching (HTTP 999).
- **Spencer Waxman's personal LinkedIn**: not found.
- **Jericho AUM**: the figure is a third-party estimate (QuiverQuant from Form ADV), not the SEC IAPD record itself.
- **Wellington fund closure**: inferred from stale data and the fund's absence from wellington.com. I found no liquidation notice.
- **Man Innovation Equity Alternative closure**: taken from a snippet of the semi-annual report PDF; I did not open the PDF to confirm the wording.
- **Schroders page "fund size USD 250,530"**: the browser run reported this, but it is clearly a share-class or price-field misread. I used the finanzen.ch fund-level figure instead.
