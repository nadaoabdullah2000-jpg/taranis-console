6. SWOT and recommendations

Strengths

  • An ownable data asset: a market-sentiment signal running since 2016
  • CEO with recurring BFM Business TV airtime
  • Award history and a clear AI-disruption thesis

Weaknesses

  • Small following; company page only reshares TV clips
  • Recent posts are French-only
  • Product page shows no performance, sleeves or team; the award label says "Managed Futures"

Opportunities

  • Close competitors are almost silent on LinkedIn
  • Monchau proves a large Swiss audience for tech and AI market charts
  • No peer publishes a proprietary sentiment read

Threats

  • BIT already tells the human + AI story with hard metrics
  • Symbit occupies "Swiss + AI + long/short"
  • Swiss marketing rules limit public performance talk
