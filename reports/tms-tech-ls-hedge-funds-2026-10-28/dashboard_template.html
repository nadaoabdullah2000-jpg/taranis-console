1. Summary

Executive summary

  • The tech long/short hedge-fund peer set is silent on LinkedIn. In 13 weeks the five company pages published one post between them (Whale Rock's intern class, 233 reactions). Cadian, Atreides and Alkeon have never posted on their pages; Sylebra last posted in 2023. Founders are silent too: Gavin Baker 1 post, Bannasch, Gibson and Sacerdote 0.
  • Atreides is not the active exception the brief expected. Its page has 0 indexed posts ever and a live search found none; Gavin Baker's reach comes from other people's channels (podcasts, NVIDIA GTC, Sohn), not his own posts. Only Coatue (4.85 a week) and Altimeter (2.23) post regularly, and they are benchmarks, not peers.
  • The lane is open, and Taranis already posts more than the peers. Taranis's page (2 posts) and Julien Leegenhoek (posting again since 23 Sep on BFM and RTS) beat every focus fund. Nobody publishes an AI-disruption or market-sentiment thesis; no direct Swiss peer exists in this set.
  • Peer sites are minimal but honest about what they are; TMS's page undersells. Cadian and Whale Rock run one-paragraph holding pages, Atreides shows its full team, Alkeon a VC portfolio wall. The TMS page sits behind a 3-step gate, then shows no fund facts, team or the two sleeves, and its award still reads "Managed Futures".
  • Four of five pass the hard filter, Sylebra is at risk. Sylebra's site now presents "Sylebra Growth Partners", a growth-equity firm; its hedge fund appears only in Form ADV data as of 31 Dec 2025. Alkeon (UCITS fund and registered sub-advisory) and Whale Rock (long-only vehicles) are kept on their private hedge funds only.

Caveats

  • The Crustdata index ends around 17 Sep 2026. A live refresh with TinyFish search found public posts by Taranis and Julien Leegenhoek only; LinkedIn's login wall blocked fetches of the Atreides and Whale Rock pages and Gavin Baker's profile, so recent posts there cannot be fully ruled out. Logged-out pages show comments, not reaction totals.
  • Hedge-fund sites are holding or login pages by design; that is recorded as a finding. Form ADV facts come from ADV aggregators (privatefunddata, Radient, formds, FINTRX) because adviserinfo.sec.gov was unreachable from this environment; official links are given by CRD number.
  • AUM sources conflict for every fund (regulatory vs discretionary vs 13F vs press); each is shown with its source, none is resolved.
  • Whale Rock was more active just before the window (posts on 18 May, 2 Jun, 9 Jun), so 13 weeks understates its usual pace. Index dates drift: its intern post is dated 12 Aug in the index but 3 Aug by its post ID.
  • Alkeon's founder has no findable LinkedIn profile. Monchau figures are from the 27 Oct counts (1,051 posts in the window); his 290k+ followers come from a search snippet and are unverified.

7. SWOT and operating plan

Strengths

  • The only fund in the set with a behavioural-sentiment quant engine (since 2016) beside discretionary tech picking
  • Founder-CEO with BFM Business and RTS airtime, already posting again
  • Already posts more than all five peers; HFM award history

Weaknesses

  • 0.15 posts a week on the page; posts mostly in French
  • Product page hides fund facts, team and the two sleeves; award labelled "Managed Futures"
  • Far smaller than peers (USD 2.7–25bn regulatory AUM)

Opportunities

  • The tech long/short peer set is silent on LinkedIn: the lane is open
  • No one publishes an AI-disruption or market-sentiment thesis
  • No direct Swiss peer in the set

Threats

  • Founders like Gavin Baker win reach through podcasts and conferences without posting
  • Coatue and Altimeter set the bar for how a tech fund communicates
  • FinSA limits public performance talk
