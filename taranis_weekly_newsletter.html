The Capacity of Equity Anomalies
Source: SSRN — Read Full Paper →
This paper measures the capacity of equity anomalies: how much capital investors can deploy before trading costs eliminate market-hedged returns above cash. Using stock-day estimates of spreads, price impact, and liquidity replenishment, They compute capacity for 35 anomalies. Alpha alone is an incomplete guide to scale. The highest-alpha long legs each support less than $2 billion, while lower-alpha profitability strategies support $ 20-33 billion. Price impact drives most cross-strategy dispersion through turnover and stock liquidity. Slower rebalancing changes the economics: median factor capacity rises thirteenfold, and the value-weighted machine-learning strategy expands from $177 million to $19 billion through partial adjustment alone.
